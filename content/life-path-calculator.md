Find your life path number free with this life path calculator. Enter your date of birth and get your life path number, your mulank and your destiny number — in seconds, free, with no sign-up.

## What Is a Life Path Number?

A life path number is the single digit you get when you reduce your full date of birth to one number. It is the first number any numerologist works out, and in numerology it is read as your life's theme: the talents you were born with, the lessons you keep meeting, and the kind of life that fits you. Your life path number never changes — it is fixed by your birth date, while your daily rashifal changes every day. The Rashifal Today page gives you the daily reading; your life path number gives you the long view.

Astrology life path readings pair the number with the chart: your life path number is read alongside your kundali and your zodiac signs — the number says what your life's theme is, the chart says how it plays out. Your life path number comes from your date of birth; your birth chart adds the time and place — the Birth Chart Calculator page draws it. Your life path number also colours your love horoscope for the year, and it is read alongside love compatibility by zodiac sign when two people are compared. The number is a guide, not a verdict: it describes tendencies, and your choices still decide your life.

## How to Calculate Life Path Number

How to calculate life path number? Add up every digit of your date of birth, then keep adding the digits of the result until you get a single digit — except for the master numbers 11, 22 and 33, which are not reduced. A life path number calculator does this in one step, but the method is simple enough to do by hand. How to calculate life path number, step by step: write your birth date as numbers, add all the digits together, and if the result has two digits, add those two digits together. Repeat until you have one digit. For 14-03-2001: 1+4+0+3+2+0+0+1 = 11 — a master number, so it stays 11 and is not reduced to 2. For 29-07-1998: 2+9+0+7+1+9+9+8 = 45, then 4+5 = 9. Life path 10 works the same way: 10 reduces to 1, so a life path number 10 is read as a 1, with the extra energy of the 0. The tool above shows each step of the sum, so you can see exactly how your number was reached.

## Life Path Number Meanings: 1 to 9

Every life path number has its own meaning. These are the traditional readings — tendencies, not rules.

- **Life path number 1 (and life path 10):** leadership and independence. 1s are read as self-starters who want to lead and do things their own way. Life path 10 is read as a 1, because 10 reduces to 1.
- **Life path number 2:** cooperation and sensitivity. 2s are read as peacemakers — diplomatic, gentle, and good at working with others.
- **Life path number 3:** creativity and expression. 3s are read as expressive and social, happiest when creating or communicating.
- **Life path number 4:** stability and hard work. 4s are read as builders — practical, patient, and steady.
- **Life path number 5:** freedom and change. 5s are read as restless and curious, happiest with variety and movement.
- **Life path number 6:** responsibility and care. 6s are read as the carers — home, family and looking after others matter most.
- **Life path number 7:** analysis and spirituality. 7s are read as thinkers — private, analytical, and drawn to deeper questions.
- **Life path number 8:** ambition and money. 8s are read as achievers, focused on success, status and material results.
- **Life path number 9:** compassion and completion. A life path number 9 is read as the humanitarian — generous, idealistic, and drawn to serving others. 9 is the number of completion: the last single digit, so 9s are read as old souls finishing a cycle.

Master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are not reduced: 11 is read as intuition and inspiration, 22 as big building and practical vision, 33 as teaching and healing. If your number is 11, 22 or 33, the tool keeps it as it is and reads both the master number and its reduced digit.

## Mulank: Your Root Number

In Indian numerology, your mulank is your root number — the number of your birth day. What is my mulank? Add the digits of the day you were born: born on the 29th, 2+9 = 11, then 1+1 = 2, so your mulank is 2. How to find mulank number is that simple — the day alone, not the month or year. The mulank is read as your core nature: how you think and act day to day, while the bhagyank (your destiny number, from the full date) is read as your life's direction.

Mulank 2 is the most searched mulank, so it gets its own section below. 2 mulank means the same thing as mulank 2 — "2 mulank" is simply the number written first. Mulank compatibility is the comparison of two people's mulanks: mulank compatibility is read for friendships, business and marriage, and it is read alongside thirumana porutham when a match is checked for marriage — Kundali Matching does the same check with the 8-koota system. The same numbers are also read for marriage timing on the When Will I Get Married page. Once you know your life path number, the Love Language Test shows how you express love day to day — the number says what you need, the love languages say how you show it.

### Mulank 2: Personality, Love Life and Compatibility

Mulank 2 personality: 2s are ruled by the Moon, and mulank 2 personality is read as gentle, sensitive and diplomatic. People with mulank 2 are said to be good listeners, peacemakers and loyal friends — they feel things deeply and they avoid conflict. Mulank 2 love life is read the same way: in love, 2s are caring and emotional, and they need reassurance and closeness from their partner. A mulank 2 love life works best with a partner who is steady and openly affectionate. Mulank 2 compatibility is traditionally read as strongest with mulank 1, mulank 2 and mulank 7 — numbers that balance the 2's softness with steadiness or understanding. Mulank 7 compatibility is read the same way for 7s: 7s are traditionally said to match well with 1, 2 and 5. Mulank 9 love life is different again: 9s are compassionate and idealistic in love, and they need a partner who shares their values — a mulank 9 love life is read as generous and devoted, but 9s can give too much and forget their own needs.

## Bhagyank and Destiny Number

Bhagyank is the Indian name for the destiny number: "bhagya" means destiny, so bhagyank is your destiny number, calculated from your full date of birth. Bhagyank calculation is the same sum as the life path number — add all the digits of your birth date and reduce to one digit. A destiny number calculator does exactly this sum, and the destiny number it gives you is your bhagyank. What is destiny number in numerology? It is the number of your life's direction: while the mulank (from the day) is read as your nature, the destiny number (from the full date) is read as where your life is headed. Destiny number meaning is that direction — the work, the relationships and the lessons your life keeps bringing you. Numerology destiny number readings use the same digits as the life path number, so your destiny number and your life path number are the same number, under two names.

How to calculate destiny number is the same as how to calculate life path number: add the digits of your full birth date and reduce to a single digit. How to find destiny number is the same sum — the tool above shows it step by step. Destiny number 9 is the most talked-about destiny number: a destiny number 9 is read as the humanitarian path — service, compassion and finishing what others start. Destiny number 2 is read as the path of cooperation and partnership — 2s build their life through relationships. Bhagyank 9 is the same as destiny number 9, and bhagyank 9 wale log — people with bhagyank 9 — are said to be compassionate, generous and drawn to helping others, sometimes at their own expense. If you want a deeper reading of your numbers together, a life path number numerologist can read your life path number, mulank and destiny number as one chart — the tool above gives you the numbers, and the Numerology Calculator page gives you the full number reading, including your lucky numbers.
