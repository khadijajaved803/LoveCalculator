## What Is a Birth Chart?

What is a birth chart? A birth chart is a snapshot of the sky — the positions of the sun, moon and planets — at the exact moment and place you were born. What are birth charts used for? In astrology, the chart is read as a map of your personality, your strengths, your relationships and the timing of events in your life. In Vedic astrology a birth chart is a kundali — your janam kundali — and the reading of it is the same whether you call it a chart or a kundali. A birth chart reading, also called a natal chart reading, explains what each placement means: which sign your sun is in, which sign your moon is in, which sign was rising, and which house each planet sits in. A birth chart analysis goes further — it weighs the whole chart together, and a free birth chart analysis from the tool above gives you that reading without paying an astrologer. A birth chart prediction is the timing part of the reading: what the chart says about the year ahead. The tool gives you a free birth chart prediction as part of the analysis, and the same reading works as horoscope birth chart — your horoscope, drawn from your own chart instead of a generic sun-sign column. Birth chart astrology is simply astrology done from the chart: the chart is the source, and everything else — your horoscope, your compatibility, your predictions — is read from it.

Your chart has three layers. The sun sign is the sign the sun was in on your birthday. The moon sign is the sign the moon was in — in Vedic astrology this is your Rasi, and the Rashifal Today page gives you the daily reading for it. And the rising sign — your ascendant — is the sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at your birth time. Together they are the big three of any chart, and the tool shows all three at the top of your result.

## Your Rising Sign and Ascendant

Your rising sign is the sign on the eastern horizon when you were born, and in astrology it is called your ascendant. Your rising sign is one of the twelve zodiac signs. What is my rising sign? It is the sign that was rising at your birth time — and what is your rising sign is the same question asked about anyone. The ascendant sign is the first house of your chart: it sets the shape of the whole chart, because the houses are counted from it. What is ascendant sign, exactly? The ascendant is the degree of the zodiac rising in the east at the moment of birth, and your ascendant sign is the sign that degree falls in.

If you want to know how to find ascendant sign, the answer is your birth time: without the time there is no ascendant, because the rising sign changes about every two hours. How to know your rising sign without a tool? You would need an ephemeris and the exact time — easier to use the ascendant calculator above. How to find rising sign on your own chart? Look at the first house: the sign on its cusp is your rising sign. Find my ascendant sign is the most common way people ask, and what is my ascendant sign and my ascendant sign are the same search: the tool answers all of them from your birth details. How to tell someone's rising sign? You cannot tell it from their face or their sun sign — you need their birth time. In ascendant astrology, the ascendant describes how you come across and how you meet the world, while the sun describes your core and the moon describes your feelings. A zodiac rising sign reading is the same as an ascendant reading — "rising sign" and "ascendant" are two names for the same thing, and an ascendant sign calculator and a rising sign calculator are the same tool.

## Lagna Chart

A lagna chart is the Vedic name for the chart drawn from your ascendant: "lagna" means ascendant, so a lagna chart is your ascendant chart. The astrology lagna chart is the first chart any Vedic astrologer draws, and a lagna chart calculator makes it from your birth time and place. The lagna chart houses are the 12 houses counted from the ascendant — the first house is you, the second is money, the seventh is marriage, the tenth is career — and the houses chart is the same 12-house wheel drawn from the lagna. How to read lagna chart? Start with the lagna itself: the sign in the first house and the planet ruling it. Then read the planets house by house. A lagna chart online view and a lagna chart generator both draw the same wheel; this tool gives you the lagna chart with the planets placed and the houses labelled. Your lagna chart is fixed at birth — it is not a today lagna chart that changes every day. The lagna in your birth chart stays the same for your whole life; what changes day to day is the transit, not the birth chart.

## Navamsa Chart and the D9 Chart

What is navamsa chart? The navamsa chart is the ninth divisional chart of Vedic astrology, and it is so important that it has its own name: the D9 chart. Navamsa chart means the chart of the ninth division — each sign is divided into nine parts, and the navamsa chart meaning is the deeper strength of the planets: a planet that is strong in both the main chart and the navamsa is strong in reality. What is D9 chart? It is the same navamsa chart — D9 is the number, navamsa is the name — and a D9 chart calculator draws it from your birth details. A navamsa chart calculator and a d9 chart calculator are the same tool, and this page's tool draws both the main chart and the D9 chart together.

A navamsa chart analysis looks at the planets in the D9: which planets are exalted, which are in their own sign, and whether the lagna lord is strong. A d9 chart analysis is the same reading. Navamsa chart predictions are traditionally read for marriage and for the second half of life — a free navamsa chart prediction on this page covers the marriage reading, and a free navamsa chart prediction for marriage looks specifically at the seventh house of the D9 and the position of Venus and Jupiter. How to read D9 chart? Check the lagna of the D9 first, then the seventh house, then whether the planets that look weak in the main chart gain strength here. A D9 navamsa chart is read side by side with the main chart, never alone. The navamsa calculator on this page also finds your pushkar navamsa — a pushkar navamsa calculator check that marks the especially auspicious degrees in the navamsa — and the D9 chart in astrology is the standard second chart of every Vedic reading, after the main chart. Navamsa, on its own, is the word for the ninth part — and the d9 chart meaning is the same as the navamsa chart meaning: the hidden strength behind the main chart.

A vedic astrology birth chart is drawn on the sidereal zodiac — the fixed stars — while a western birth chart is drawn on the tropical zodiac, the seasons. A vedic birth chart calculator draws the sidereal chart, and a free vedic birth chart from the tool above comes with the Vedic reading: lagna, navamsa and divisional charts included.

## Houses, Planets and Shadbala

Your houses chart — the 12 houses — is where the reading happens. Each house rules one area of life: the first is you, the second is money, the fourth is home, the fifth is children and creativity, the seventh is marriage, the tenth is career. The planets in each house colour that area: Jupiter in the tenth is read for career luck, Venus in the seventh for marriage. A birth chart analysis weighs the houses together with the signs and the divisional charts, and a free birth chart analysis from the tool gives you that whole reading.

Shadbala is the measure of a planet's strength. A shadbala calculator works out six kinds of strength for each planet and adds them into one score. What is shadbala? Shadbala means "sixfold strength", and shadbala in astrology is the standard way to say whether a planet can deliver its results: a planet with high shadbala is strong, one with low shadbala is weak even if it sits in a good sign. Shadbala meaning is simply that sixfold strength, and the tool shows the shadbala score beside each planet in your chart.
