### 1. Dina Porutham

Dina porutham checks day-to-day well-being: whether the stars of the couple promise a healthy, steady married life. "Dina" means day, so dina porutham is the day porutham — the stars are counted from the bride's star to the groom's, and certain counts are considered good for daily life together. If dina porutham does not match, the traditional reading is that everyday life together will face more friction, so families treat it as an important porutham for marriage, though not one of the two must-pass ones.

### 2. Gana Porutham

Gana porutham checks temperament: whether the two natures get along. Every birth star belongs to one of three ganas — Deva (divine), Manushya (human) or Rakshasa (demon) — and gana porutham compares them. Gana porutham meaning in tamil is exactly this: the porutham of the couple's ganas, their natures. A Deva–Deva or Manushya–Manushya pairing is considered a good gana porutham; a Rakshasa pairing with a Deva star is traditionally considered a mismatch. Is gana porutham important for marriage? Yes — temperament is what a couple lives with every day, so gana porutham importance is high even though it is not a must-pass porutham on its own. Gana porutham in tamil is the same check, asked in Tamil.

### 3. Mahendra Porutham

Mahendra porutham checks progeny and a long married life. It is calculated from the stars — the groom's star counted from the bride's — and a matching mahendra porutham is traditionally read as a sign of children and a lasting marriage. Families who ask for a mahendra porutham check are asking about the future of the family, not just the couple, which is why it sits near the top of the important porutham for marriage list.

### 4. Sthree Deergha Porutham

Sthree deergha porutham checks the wife's well-being and long life. "Sthree" means woman and "deergha" means long, so stree deergha porutham — also written stree porutham or deergha porutham — is the porutham of the wife's long life. It is calculated from the groom's star counted from the bride's, and a matching stree deergha porutham is traditionally read as a sign of the wife's health and longevity. What is stree deergha porutham, asked plainly, is answered here: it is the longevity porutham for the bride. If stree deergha porutham is not matching, families take it seriously — it is one of the poruthams that is read carefully even when the overall count is high.

### 5. Yoni Porutham

Yoni porutham checks physical compatibility. Every birth star has an animal symbol (its yoni), and yoni porutham compares the two animals — friendly animals match, hostile ones do not. A yoni porutham calculator and a yoni porutham chart are the same check written two ways: the calculator finds the animals from the stars, the chart lists them side by side. It is the porutham of closeness and physical harmony between the couple.

### 6. Rasi Porutham

Rasi porutham checks moon-sign compatibility: whether the bride's rasi and the groom's rasi get along. Rasi porutham is the rasi half of the match — the two moon signs are counted from each other, and certain counts are traditionally considered unfavourable unless the rasi lords are friendly, which is where the next porutham comes in. ### 7. Rasi Adhipathi Porutham

Rasi adhipathi porutham checks the friendship of the lords of the two moon signs. "Adhipathi" means lord, so this is the porutham of the rasi lords: if the lord of the bride's rasi and the lord of the groom's rasi are friends, the rasi porutham is considered good even when the raw rasi count is not ideal. It is the porutham that rescues a weak rasi match, which is why it is always read together with rasi porutham, never alone.

### 8. Vasya Porutham

Vasya porutham checks mutual attraction and influence: whether each partner naturally draws the other. "Vasya" means control or attraction, so vasya porutham is the porutham of attraction — the stars are read for whether the couple will influence each other kindly and stay drawn to each other. A matching vasya porutham is traditionally read as a sign of a close, affectionate marriage.

### 9. Rajju Porutham

Rajju porutham checks the longevity of the marriage itself — and it is one of the two must-pass poruthams. "Rajju" means rope or knot, and the check looks at five rajju knots, from the feet to the head, to see whether the couple's stars fall in the same knot. If both stars fall in the same rajju, the rajju porutham fails, and the traditional reading is that the marriage itself will not last — which is why a rajju porutham calculator is often run on its own, before the other nine. Vedha porutham checks for obstructions between the stars — and it is the other must-pass porutham. "Vedha" means obstruction or piercing, so vedha porutham is the porutham of obstructions: certain star pairs are said to obstruct each other, and if the couple's stars are one of those pairs, the vedha porutham fails. ## Which Poruthams Matter Most?

Not all 10 poruthams carry the same weight. The important porutham for marriage list, as Tamil families traditionally read it, is:

- **Must pass, no exceptions in practice:** Rajju Porutham and Vedha Porutham. If either fails, the match is not recommended — the count of the other eight does not rescue it.
- **Read carefully:** Dina, Gana, Mahendra and Sthree Deergha. A mismatch here is taken seriously, and families often look for a second opinion or a remedy before proceeding.
- **Support the verdict:** Yoni, Rasi, Rasi Adhipathi and Vasya. These shape how happy and close the marriage will be, and a strong showing here can balance a weak count elsewhere — but only when Rajju and Vedha pass.

As a rough guide, a match with 9 or 10 poruthams and both must-pass poruthams clear is considered excellent; 7 or 8 with Rajju and Vedha clear is considered good; 6 or fewer, or any failure of Rajju or Vedha, is traditionally not recommended. The count is a guide, not a verdict on its own — always read it together with the Rajju and Vedha rows, which is exactly what the tool above shows.
